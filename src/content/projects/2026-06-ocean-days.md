The zine started as a sketchbook study of the tide coming in. Twenty-four pages,
two risograph colors, hand-stitched binding with waxed thread. Each copy got a
slightly different cover, because ink coverage always surprises you.

## Process notes

- Sketched on location, traced into vectors
- Printed in sea-foam + rust on 70gsm Munken
- Bound with the three-hole pamphlet stitch
