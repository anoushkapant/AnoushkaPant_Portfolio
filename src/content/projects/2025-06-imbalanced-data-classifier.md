A machine-learning project tackling one of the trickiest real-world problems:
**highly imbalanced classification**.

## Approach

- Designed and implemented a feedforward **neural network** tailored for highly imbalanced data (Pima Indians Diabetes dataset)
- Evaluated against **SVM** and other classifiers
- Experimented with sampling techniques and hyperparameters to optimize **accuracy and recall**

## Outcome

Systematic comparison of sampling + tuning strategies, with clear documentation
of the trade-offs between precision and recall on a skewed dataset.
