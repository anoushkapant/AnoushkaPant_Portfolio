Selected as a **Top 5 finalist (out of 2,000+ participants)** at Cathay's
Hackathon 2025.

## What we built

Co-developed an interactive internal dashboard that automates quotation
generation for airfreight and trucking, incorporating external cost factors and
simulated data.

- Focused on intuitive UI/UX so non-technical users could make faster, data-driven decisions
- Prototyped and iterated the flow with the judging panel in mind, keeping the demo tight and the interface clear

## Key takeaway

Pressure-testing a product idea end to end in 24 hours — from problem framing
to a working demo — was the best crash course in scoping and teamwork.
